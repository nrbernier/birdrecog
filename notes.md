## Idea

I want to make a game to help learn to recognize bird species. Some kind of
duolingo for birds that gamifies the process.

I want it to be based on species found in Switzerland (ideally 20-50 species
common). One should be asked both to recognize the birds based on how they look
and on their sounds.

It should be possible to switch to name the birds either in english or in
french.

The game should be phone-friendy.
